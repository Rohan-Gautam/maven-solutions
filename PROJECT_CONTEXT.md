# Maven Solutions Website Context

Maven Solutions is a specialty chemistry company serving pulp and paper manufacturing, with additional ETP chemical-program capability for textile manufacturing. ## Flagship Technologies / Programs

### REDRAMAX: Advance Retention and Drainage Program

- Focus: balancing retention, drainage and formation for paper makers; applicable to kraft, writing-printing and specialty paper producers.
- Described approach: dispersion/fixation of fibre, colloidal and nano-particle material to manage fines/fines-filler complex/colloidal matter in process water.
- Components: MS-NEROCOL C (post screen, 0.5-1 kg/ton), MS-BENPRO (post screen, 1-3 kg/ton), PAC/Alum (thin stock, 2-3 kg/ton, if needed), and selected cationic/anionic polyacrylamide (post screen, 0.1-0.2 kg/ton). Programme selection is based on lab evaluation and system charge analysis.
- Source: *REDRAMAX - Advance Retention And Drainage Program.pdf*.

### FIBRAMAX / FIBRA: Advance Filler Retention Program

- Focus: filler retention efficiency in paper manufacturing via a grafted biopolymer.
- Described approach: controlled filler agglomeration and incorporation into the fibre structure, minimising disruptive fibre-filler interactions.
- Documented benefits: supports filler utilisation and stable paper properties while preserving fibre bonding, strength and sheet structure.
- Application: prepare a 2% lump-free solution, heat to 45-65 C with agitation, and add near the filler-addition point in the wet end. Stated dosage: 300-500 kg per ton of paper (shown as supplied; technical clarification recommended before use).
- Properties: low viscosity; pH 7 (+/- 1); ash not more than 2%; loss on drying not more than 12%; minimum 12-month shelf life in good warehouse conditions.
- Source: *FIBRAMAX - Advance Filler Retention Program.pdf*.
