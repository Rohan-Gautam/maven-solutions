WHAT DOES TCMP COVER?
1. Complete Chemical Requirement Management
Maven Solutions can take responsibility for managing your complete range of
process chemicals. From individual specialty chemicals to your entire
chemical basket, we work towards simplifying procurement and creating a
single, technically capable partner for your chemical requirements.
We begin by understanding your existing chemical program.
Our technical team evaluates:
Chemicals currently being used
Consumption and dosage
Chemical costs
Product performance
Application methods
Process conditions
This gives us a clear picture of where your chemical spend and process
inefficiencies are coming from.
2. Chemical Program Audit
Chemical compatibility
Quality and consistency
Existing suppliers
Inventory and logistics
Opportunities for optimization
A chemical that is cheaper per kilogram is not necessarily cheaper for your
process.
We therefore focus on cost per unit of production and actual process
performance, rather than simply comparing product prices.
Our approach may include:
Product optimization
Dosage reduction
Product substitution
Multi-product optimization
3. Product & Application Optimization
Improved application techniques
Process-condition optimization
New chemical technologies
Supplier consolidation
4. Cost Reduction
Where possible, we work towards reducing your total chemical expenditure
without compromising product quality or process performance.
Our benchmark:
Target → Minimum 10% reduction in chemical program cost
This can be achieved through better product economics, reduced
consumption, improved procurement, optimized dosage, or a combination of
these factors.

5. Chemical Performance Improvement
Cost reduction is only one part of the equation.
In many cases, the bigger opportunity is to obtain better results from the
same or lower quantity of chemicals.
Our target is therefore also:
Target → Minimum 10% improvement in chemical Performance
Depending on the industry and application, this could mean:
Lower chemical consumption
Higher process efficiency
Improved product quality
Reduced wastage
Better machine performance
Reduced downtime
Improved retention or yield
Lower rejects
Better end-product properties
6. Technical Support & Continuous Monitoring
TCPM doesn't end after implementation.
Maven Solutions continuously works with your technical and production
teams to monitor the chemical program and identify further opportunities.

WHY MAVEN SOLUTIONS?
We Don't Just Supply Chemicals. We Manage the Program.
Traditional chemical procurement often focuses on one question:
“What is the price per kilogram?”
TCPM asks a much more important question:
“What is the total cost and performance of the chemical program?”
A product that costs 10% less but requires 20% more dosage is not a saving.
Similarly, a product that costs more but significantly improves process
efficiency may actually reduce your total operating cost.
TCPM looks at the complete picture.
