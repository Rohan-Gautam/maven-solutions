MS - BENTONITE POWDER
(INSTANT HYDRATING EXTENDED SODIUM ALUMINO SILICATE)
DESCRIPTION -
It is characterised by high ion-exchange-capacity and extremely high active
surface area in aqueous dispersions as carrier of both negative and positive
charge. MS - BENTONITE POWDER have properties for dispersion and
absorption Filler/Fiber Filler Complex/Colloidal particles hence contribute in
better drainage. MS - BENTONITE POWDER is widely applied as effective
additive for sticky and deposit control, in order to control and reduce the
detrimental effect of various contaminants, occurring in the paper making
process.
POTENTIAL BENEFITS -
Reduces Scaling Potential
Prevents CaCO₃ and Mg(OH)₂ precipitation by binding free Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺.
Flocculates & Settles Suspended Solids
Bentonite works as a coagulant aid in combination with PAC, alum, or cationic
polymers, improving removal of fines, fillers, starch residues, and dyes.
Traps Hydrophobic Contaminants
Effectively adsorbs stickies, pitch, resins, and anionic trash, reducing organic
buildup in process water. Enhances felt and wire cleanliness, improving
runnability.
Improves White Water Clarity
Helps in reducing COD/BOD by removing dissolved organic load. Reduces
foaming caused by excess dissolved organics.
Improves Retention of Fines & Fillers
Works synergistically with PEO, C-PAM, and microparticle systems (silica,
bentonite) to improve retention of fibers, fines, and fillers (CaCO₃, TiO₂, clay).
Faster Drainage & Water Removal
Bentonite forms microflocs that enhance water drainage, leading to higher
dewatering efficiency and faster machine speeds.
Improves Effluent Clarity
Lowers Turbidity and suspended solids in discharge water.
