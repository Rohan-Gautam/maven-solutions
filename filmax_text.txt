APPLICATION
Dissolve desired quantity of our specialised filler program FILMAX PLUS+ (Includes -
FILMAX FILLER + MS - BINDMAX [2% of FILMAX FILLER]) in fresh or Treated back
water and make a lump free solution. The ready solution can be added to thick or thin
stock. Select a suitable dosing point. Like other fillers the above solution must be used
in an agitated tank to prevent settling. The above combination can be used as dry
basis before refining, ensuring proper mixing the above with the pulp.
SYSTEM REQUIREMENT
Preparation tank of 3-5 m3 capacity with adequate agitator for preparing FILMAX
slurry,
Service tank of 3-5 m3 capacity with adequate agitator along with dosing pump
having capacity of 1000 - 2000LPH.
AREA OF APPLICATION
Can be applied at multiple locations in the paper making process but the most
preferable area is refining chest/mixing chest/machine chest.
